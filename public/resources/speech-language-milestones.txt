Speech and Language Milestone Notes

Seek professional guidance if a child:
- Does not respond consistently to sound.
- Has limited babbling or words compared with peers.
- Struggles to follow simple instructions.
- Is difficult for familiar listeners to understand.
- Loses previously learned speech or language skills.

Early support can make home communication easier and more consistent.
