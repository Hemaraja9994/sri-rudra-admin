Parent Home Practice Guide

Simple ways to support communication at home:
1. Follow the child's interest during play.
2. Model short, clear phrases.
3. Wait and give the child time to respond.
4. Expand what the child says by adding one or two words.
5. Read aloud daily and talk about pictures.
6. Keep practice short, warm, and consistent.
