Hearing Care Guide

1. Keep hearing aids dry and away from moisture.
2. Clean ear moulds and domes gently as advised by your audiologist.
3. Store devices in their case when not in use.
4. Replace batteries or recharge devices before long outings.
5. Schedule follow-up programming if speech sounds unclear or loud environments feel uncomfortable.
